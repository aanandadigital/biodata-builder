1. Information we collect

We collect information in three ways: what you type into the builder, what you upload, and what's needed to process a payment.

a) Biodata content you enter

  • Name, date of birth, height, weight, education, profession, and similar personal details
  • Family details (e.g. parents' names/occupations, siblings) if your chosen template includes them
  • Contact details used for delivery: full name, email address, and phone number

b) Photos

Any photo you upload to place on your biodata (profile photo or additional photos, depending on the template).

c) Payment & order details

Your name, email, phone number, selected template, and order amount are sent to our backend to create an order and, after payment, to generate and email your PDF. We never see or store your card, UPI, or bank details — those are handled entirely by Razorpay (see Payments below).

2. How your draft biodata is stored

While you're filling in the form, your in-progress biodata is auto-saved as a draft in your own browser's local storage, so you don't lose your work if you close the tab or refresh. This draft stays on your device — it is not sent to our servers until you click "Pay & Download" and complete checkout. Clearing your browser data, or using "Start over" in the builder, removes this local draft.

3. How we use your information

  • To render the live preview of your biodata as you type
  • To generate your final, print-ready PDF after payment is confirmed
  • To email the finished PDF to the address you provide
  • To process payment and prevent fraud (via Razorpay)
  • To respond to support requests
  • To enforce our Terms of Service, including the minimum-age requirement on marriage biodata templates

We do not use your biodata content or photos for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Photo uploads

Photos you upload are used only to render your biodata and are transmitted to our backend at the point you generate/download a PDF. Please only upload photos you have the right to use.

5. Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay, a licensed third-party payment gateway. When you pay, Razorpay collects and processes your payment details directly under its own privacy policy and PCI-DSS compliant systems. We only receive confirmation that payment succeeded, along with an order reference — never your full card, UPI, or bank account details.

6. Sharing & disclosure

We share information only where necessary to run the service:

  • Razorpay — to process your payment
  • Our hosting/infrastructure providers — to run the application and generate your PDF (currently a Railway-hosted backend and Vercel-hosted frontend)
  • Email delivery services — to send your finished PDF to your inbox
  • Law enforcement or regulators — only if legally required to do so

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

7. Data retention

We retain order records (name, email, phone, template used, payment confirmation) for as long as needed to deliver your PDF, handle support or refund requests, and meet our tax/accounting obligations. Draft data that never reaches checkout stays only in your own browser's local storage and is never sent to us. You can request deletion of your order records at any time — see Your rights below.

8. Your rights & choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your personal information from our records
  • Withdraw consent to future communications from us

To exercise any of these, email us — see Contact us. We'll respond within a reasonable time, generally within 30 days.

9. Cookies & local storage

We keep things minimal:

  • Theme preference (light/dark mode) is saved in local storage so it persists on your next visit.
  • In-progress biodata drafts are saved in local storage as described in section 2.
  • The Razorpay checkout script may set its own cookies while the payment window is open, governed by Razorpay's own policy.

We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

10. Children's privacy

The Biodata Builder is not directed at children. Marriage-category templates include a built-in age check and require the date of birth entered to indicate an age of 18 or older. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we'll remove it.

11. Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access to backend systems — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data appropriately for the type of information involved.
