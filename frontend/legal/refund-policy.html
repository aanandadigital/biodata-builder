Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026 • Last updated: August 10, 2026

Because a biodata PDF is generated and delivered digitally within moments of payment, most purchases are final once the PDF has been successfully delivered. This page explains exactly when a refund does and doesn't apply.

1. Before you pay

You can cancel at any time before completing payment — simply close the payment window or leave the page. No charge is made unless a payment is fully completed via Razorpay, and nothing is owed if you cancel beforehand.

2. Eligible for a refund

You are eligible for a full refund if:

  • Payment was successfully deducted from your account, but your PDF was not generated or delivered within 24 hours, and our support team is unable to resolve the delivery within a further 48 hours
  • You were charged more than once for the same order due to a technical error
  • The PDF you received is corrupted, blank, or fails to open, and we're unable to promptly re-send a working copy

3. Not eligible for a refund

Refunds are generally not available once your PDF has been successfully generated and delivered to your email, including if:

  • You entered incorrect personal details, family details, or a wrong photo, and the PDF reflects exactly what you typed
  • You simply changed your mind about the template or design after downloading
  • You want a different template after already receiving your PDF (you're welcome to start a new, separately paid order for a different template)
  • You entered an incorrect email address and the PDF could not be delivered as a result — please double check your email before paying

4. How to request a refund

Email aananda.creation@gmail.com with:

  • Your Razorpay payment ID or order ID (from your payment confirmation)
  • The email address used at checkout
  • A short description of the issue, and a screenshot if relevant

We aim to respond within 2–3 business days. If your request is approved, refunds are issued to your original payment method via Razorpay and typically reflect within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.

5. Order cancellations after payment

Because PDF generation begins automatically as soon as payment is confirmed, we're generally unable to cancel an order after payment succeeds. If you paid by mistake (e.g. duplicate order), contact support immediately and we'll do our best to help before generation completes.

6. Contact

For anything refund-related, reach us at aananda.creation@gmail.com or visit Contact Support. See also our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
