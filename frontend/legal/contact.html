Frequently asked questions

I paid but didn't receive my PDF — what do I do?

First, check your spam/promotions folder. If it's genuinely missing after 24 hours, email us with your order ID and we'll resend it or issue a refund per our Refund Policy.

Can I edit my biodata after downloading?

Yes — reopen the same template from the gallery, re-enter your details (your last draft may still be saved in your browser), and download again. Note this creates a new, separately paid order.

Is my payment information safe?

Yes. All payments are handled directly by Razorpay; we never see or store your card, UPI, or bank details. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Do you offer marriage biodata templates for every community?

Yes — browse by category (Hindu, Muslim, Christian, Sikh, General/professional, and more) from the template gallery.
